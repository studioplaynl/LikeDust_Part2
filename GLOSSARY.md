# We All Breathe The Same Air

Interactive graphic novel telling Sam Yazdanpanna's true story of arriving in the Netherlands at seventeen, navigating the asylum system, and becoming undocumented, following *Like Dust* (Part 1).

## Language

### Spaces & Flow

**Doors**:
The two side-by-side book panels on the landing screen representing Part 1 (*Like Dust*) and Part 2 (*We All Breathe The Same Air*).
_Avoid_: Hero cards, banners, tiles

**Intro Film**:
The opening prologue video introducing Sam's departure from Iran, Istanbul, and flight to the Netherlands before the interactive story begins.
_Avoid_: Trailer, splash video

**Window Scene**:
The airport terminal window reflection bridging the Intro Film and The Room, where adult Sam looks at the flickering reflection of his seventeen-year-old self.
_Avoid_: Mirror screen, intro page, lobby

**The Room**:
Sam's shared asylum room that acts as the story's central hub, progressing visually from afternoon to dusk as Chapters are completed.
_Avoid_: Main menu, dashboard, level select

**Finale**:
The closing sequence in The Room after all four Chapters are complete: Sam's Window Gaze, the Dove flying out the window, a slow camera zoom toward the window, and the End Card.
_Avoid_: Outro, epilogue video

### Sam's Poses in The Room

**Sitting Pose**:
Sam sitting quietly on the lower bunk when entering The Room before the story begins.
_Avoid_: Bed idle, man layer

**Stand-Up**:
Sam's single opening movement when clicked on the bunk, rising from the bed and stepping into the sunbeam.
_Avoid_: Prologue clip, intro animation

**Think Pose**:
Sam's continuous standing posture in the sunbeam (hand on chin) held throughout all four Chapters until the Finale, without intermediate object-pickup or counting animations.
_Avoid_: Idle loop, reaction clip, think reaction

**Window Gaze**:
Sam's single closing movement at the start of the Finale, turning his back to look out the barred window.
_Avoid_: Standup part 2, turn animation

**Dove**:
The white bird resting on the bedpost in The Room that flies out through the window during the Finale.
_Avoid_: Pigeon

### Chapters & Narrative

**Chapter**:
One of the four interactive film episodes (*Schiphol*, *The AZC*, *Amsterdam*, *The Letter*) unlocked sequentially in The Room.
_Avoid_: Level, subpage, video clip

**Chapter Object**:
One of the four personal items in The Room (*Passport*, *Photo Frame*, *Bicycle*, *Rejection Letter*) that unlocks and opens its corresponding Chapter.
_Avoid_: Hotspot, trigger, icon

**Moment**:
A point inside a Chapter where the film pauses on a frozen frame and waits for the viewer to act before the story continues.
_Avoid_: Interaction, minigame, quiz, assignment

**Yellow Card**:
The asylum identity card generated during *Schiphol* that assigns the viewer a system number in place of Sam's name and carries forward across Chapters.
_Avoid_: Badge, profile card, ticket

**Cell Wall**:
The shared detention wall in *Schiphol* where viewers rub away darkness to read previous detainees' words and scratch their own message.
_Avoid_: Guestbook, comment board

### Text & Language

**Burned-in English**:
The original English narration text rendered directly inside the film frames of the Intro Film and Chapters.
_Avoid_: Hardsubs, default captions

**Subtitle**:
Timed Dutch (`nl`) or Farsi (`fa`) translation displayed in a dark band over the bottom of the film frame when a non-English language is active (enabled by default for `nl` and `fa`, with an optional toggle button; hidden when `en` is active).
_Avoid_: Caption, toast

**Sam's Voice**:
Sam's inner monologue appearing word-by-word over a frozen Moment frame in the active language (`en`, `nl`, or `fa`).
_Avoid_: Subtitle, prompt, tooltip

**Action Prompt**:
Short instruction telling the viewer how to engage with a Moment or scene (e.g., *Press and hold to hold your breath*, *Click young Sam to begin*).
_Avoid_: Hint, caption, subtitle

**Room Caption**:
Brief observational line appearing at the top of The Room (e.g., *Click the passport to begin.*).
_Avoid_: Subtitle, toast
