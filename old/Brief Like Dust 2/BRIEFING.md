We All Breathe the Same Air / Like dust 2

Author and director: Sam Yazdanpanna
Interaction director: Maarten van der Glas

Like Dust is the refugee story of Sam Yazdanpanna. As a teen he fled from Iran to The Netherlands. It is a multipart story. Like Dust 1 was an interactive app and website. The comic was told as a sequences of videos, with minimal interaction.

For Like Dust 2 we want to incorporate more interaction, interaction that helps tell the story and involve the audience.

As a narrative device we tell the story from the place where Sam will end up: in the room of his forster family. This is not clear yet but it brings curiosity and tension: The audience asks what the relationship between the roon and the story is. It also brings some relief because the story is quite heavy but the room is peaceful and mundain. The audience knows it will be alright with Sam but the question is how: at the end of the story we still dont know how Sam ended up in this room, so we want to know how part 3 goes...

The room and chapter bring also a contrast: heavy and dark with some hope, to peace. 

The room serves as an umbrella to hang other features: old photo's of Sam, links to the previous story.

1. 3D scene of a teens room toon shaded in the style of the comic
2. interactive elements in the room that unlock chapters of the comic
3. per chapter camera animation over the comic page
4. change in the room when we come back from an unseen chapter (eg object to unlock next chanper, light change progressing from evening to morning)

5. voice over and music/ sound
